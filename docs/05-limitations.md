# 5. Known limitations and next step
**Facts:** synthetic data; local-only (browser localStorage); no backend, auth or integration; simulated roles.
**Assumptions:** thresholds, windows, SLAs, schedules, tier order — all illustrative.
**Hypotheses:** the loop reduces ownership ambiguity and documents closure.
**Unknowns:** clinical appropriateness of rules, clinician workload effect, competitor feature gaps (benchmark sources not read directly), Arabic wording acceptance, governance/security requirements.
Other limits: glucose only; UTC-day completeness; hand-written Arabic strings not reviewed by a native clinical editor; review timer measures real elapsed seconds of a simulated activity; no accessibility audit.
**Next practical step:** a 60-minute walkthrough with 3–5 clinicians using the S1–S9 scenarios to critique the tier order and explanations, and a primary-source re-check of the benchmark.
